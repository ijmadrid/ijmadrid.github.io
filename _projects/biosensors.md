In collaboration with [Nicolas Clément](https://limms-tokyo.org/) at LIMMS, CNRS–University of Tokyo, I study the probability distributions associated with single-electron electrical measurements in DNA-based biosensors developed in [Prof. Fujii’s laboratory](http://www.microfluidics.iis.u-tokyo.ac.jp/).

These projects bring together point processes, counting statistics and stochastic models of molecular motion. Applications include noise suppression in nanotransistor biosensors and the analysis of redox-labelled biological systems.

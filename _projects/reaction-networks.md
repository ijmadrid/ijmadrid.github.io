With [Praful Gagrani](https://www.prafulgagrani.com/) and [Tetsuya Kobayashi](https://research.crmind.net/), I work on fluctuations and large deviations in stochastic chemical reaction networks, with applications to growing cells.

Our [preprint on growing chemical reaction networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30970) studies systems whose volume grows while their composition stabilises. We develop a large-deviations description of composition fluctuations and of observables accumulated during growth.

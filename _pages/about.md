I am a probabilist working on stochastic models of growth, with applications in biology. Since April 2024, I have been a postdoctoral researcher in [Tetsuya Kobayashi’s team](https://research.crmind.net/) at the Institute of Industrial Science, [The University of Tokyo](https://www.iis.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/).

I completed my Ph.D. in applied mathematics at [CMAP, École polytechnique](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/), in February 2024, under the supervision of [Sylvie Méléard](https://sites.google.com/view/sylvie-meleard/accueil) and [Meriem El Karoui](https://www.elkarouilab.fr/), within the [PEIPS group](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/en/research/probability/peips) and [Inria project-team MERGE](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/en/research/probability/merge).

My research connects the dynamics of individual cells and chemical reactions with the behaviour of growing populations. I am particularly interested in long-time behaviour, fluctuations and selection, and in the roles of individual variability and inherited memory. My work draws on branching processes, structured population models, spectral methods and large deviations.

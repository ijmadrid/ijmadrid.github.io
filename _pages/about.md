I am a probabilist applied mathematician working on stochastic models motivated by problems across different scales of living systems, from biochemistry to evolving populations. Since April 2024, I have been a postdoctoral researcher in [Tetsuya Kobayashi’s team](https://research.crmind.net/) at the Institute of Industrial Science, [The University of Tokyo](https://www.iis.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/). I completed my Ph.D. in applied mathematics at [CMAP, École polytechnique](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/), under the supervision of [Sylvie Méléard](https://sites.google.com/view/sylvie-meleard/accueil) and [Meriem El Karoui](https://www.elkarouilab.fr/), within the [PEIPS group](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/en/research/probability/peips) and [Inria project-team MERGE](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/en/research/probability/merge).

I am particularly interested in long-time behaviours, selection mechanisms, and in the roles of individual variability and phenotypic plasticity in the macroscopic properties of growing populations. My work draws on branching processes, structured population models, spectral methods and more recently, the theory of large deviations.
